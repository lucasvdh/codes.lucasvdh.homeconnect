Tired of appliances that drop offline for days? Home Connect (Local) connects your Bosch and Siemens appliances to Homey over your own network, so they keep working even when the cloud doesn't. Follow every program in real time and build Flows around the moment your appliance is done.

A one-time SingleKey ID login fetches the local keys; after that, everything stays on your network. This is an independent community app, not affiliated with BSH, Bosch or Siemens.
