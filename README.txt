Tired of appliances that drop offline for days? Home Connect (Local) talks directly to your Bosch and Siemens appliances over your own network, so your oven, dishwasher, washing machine, dryer, extractor hood and coffee machine stay responsive in Homey without depending on the cloud. Follow program progress, remaining time and door state in real time, start a program when energy is cheap, and build Flows around the moment your dishes or laundry are done.

Setup only needs a one-time login with your SingleKey ID to fetch each appliance's local keys. After that, everything runs on your local network. This is an independent community app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by BSH Hausgeräte GmbH, Bosch or Siemens.
